Projects

The US Corporate Bond Refinancing Gap

Python · FRED API

Rebuilt Bloomberg's “Pricey Refi” chart from free public data to measure how much more US companies now pay to refinance maturing debt. Forty years of Moody's Baa yields, a rolling-mean coupon proxy, and a 5/7/10-year robustness check — the gap ran at −0.78 pp through the 2010s and sits at +1.33 pp today, positive for four straight years. All series exported to Excel so the result can be verified without running the code.
